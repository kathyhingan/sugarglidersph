## What the paperwork covers

Every purchase includes the legal documentation that makes your ownership clean and registered. This is the line that separates a properly run breeder from a stall seller, and it matters: an undocumented exotic is a risk you carry for the life of the animal. You will never have to wonder about the status of a glider that came from us.

## A note on bonded pairs

Sugar gliders are colony animals. A single glider with an attentive owner can do well, but many quietly struggle alone. If you cannot give several hours of daily interaction, a bonded pair is kinder and usually easier. We will give you the straight version for your situation before you commit.
